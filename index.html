EarnCruiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Welcome to EarnCruiz. This Privacy Policy explains how EarnCruiz collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use EarnCruiz, we may process account and profile information, task activity, reward records, wallet and transaction information, withdrawal requests, and information you provide when contacting support.

The app and its third-party services may also process technical information, such as device information, diagnostics, and advertising identifiers, where applicable.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage accounts, provide app features, record task and reward activity, process and review withdrawal requests, prevent fraud, maintain security, provide support, and improve the service.

3. Backend and Service Providers

EarnCruiz uses Supabase for backend services. Information processed by the service may be stored or handled by Supabase according to the app's configuration and applicable service terms.

EarnCruiz may use advertising providers, including Google AdMob, if advertising features are enabled. Those providers may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate EarnCruiz, when needed to provide requested services, protect users, comply with legal obligations, or address fraud and security issues. We do not promise that information is never shared.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain transaction records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies. Appropriate safeguards are used to help protect information, although no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request account deletion. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and transaction purposes. Account deletion requests are subject to verification.

7. Children's Privacy

EarnCruiz is not intended for children where use of the service would be prohibited by applicable law. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as EarnCruiz changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
